Mission

Why we exist

Most organisations are not indifferent to data protection. They are unsure where they stand, uncertain which of a hundred possible actions matters most, and short of the time to find out.

Advice alone rarely resolves that. A report that nobody implements changes nothing, and training that nobody remembers changes less. So we built one route that runs from assessment through to closed gaps, trained staff and a dated attestation — with each stage producing something concrete.

Approach

How we work

Six principles that decide what goes into a report, what we recommend, and what we refuse to claim.

Evidence over assertion

A finding is only as good as what supports it. We score what we can see, and we say plainly when something cannot be evidenced.

Plain language

Reports are written to be acted on by the people who have to act on them — not to demonstrate familiarity with the legislation.

Proportionate recommendations

The right control for a 40-person firm is rarely the right control for a 4,000-person group. Recommendations are sized to the organisation.

No fear-selling

We do not quote unsourced fine figures or frighten boards into buying. The risk is real enough described accurately.

Clear about our limits

Our attestation says what it says and nothing more. We are not a regulator and we do not imply otherwise.

We assess ourselves too

We run the same assessment against our own organisation and publish our position on the trust page.
