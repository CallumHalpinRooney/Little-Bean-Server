The programme

One route from unknown risk to evidenced assurance

Six stages, run once and then maintained annually. Each stage produces something concrete: a completed assessment, a scored report, a closed gap register, trained staff, a dated attestation.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A structured questionnaire across ten data protection domains, completed by your team.

  2. 02

    Report

    Our specialists score your responses and set out the gaps in priority order.

  3. 03

    Remediate

    Close the gaps — with your own team guided by us, or with our specialists doing the work.

  4. 04

    Train

    Role-based awareness training that adapts to each employee and reports to managers.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Meet the standard and receive a dated Verified attestation for tenders and audits.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Annual reassessment keeps the attestation current as your organisation changes.

Why it matters

Four pressures, one underlying question

Regulators, customers and procurement teams are all asking the same thing in different words: can you show what you do with personal data, and evidence that it works?

Regulatory exposure

Infringements of the GDPR carry administrative fines set out in Article 83, up to the higher tiers for the most serious breaches. Supervisory authorities also have corrective powers including orders to stop processing.

Verified attestation

Evidence you can hand to a procurement team

Organisations that meet the required standard receive a dated [COMPANY NAME] Verified attestation — a digital badge for your website and a certificate for tenders, audits and customer due diligence.

What it is and is not. It confirms your organisation was independently assessed by [COMPANY NAME] against our published criteria and met the required standard on the date shown. It is not a GDPR certification under Article 42, and it is not issued, approved or endorsed by the Data Protection Commission or any supervisory authority.
